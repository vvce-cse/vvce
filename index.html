About VVCE

A heritage of learning since 1949

Vidyavardhaka College of Engineering was established in 1997 by Vidyavardhaka Sangha, an education society that has served Mysuru since 1949. The college is affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University, Belagavi, and has been autonomous since 2020–21.

1949Vidyavardhaka Sangha founded
1997VVCE opens in Gokulam
2020Autonomous status; CSE (AI&ML)
2024NAAC 'A', CGPA 3.19

The Gokulam campus is about 4 km from Mysuru railway station, with hostels for men and women, sports facilities and an active training and placement cell.
